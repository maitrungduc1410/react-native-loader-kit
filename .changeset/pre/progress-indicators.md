`LoaderKitProgress` shows the progress of a task on iOS and Android, drawn by LoaderKit: 50 designs across 10 types (linear, circular, pie, gauge, liquid, border, bars, grid, battery and hourglass), with variants such as `wavy`, `segmented`, `striped`, `gradient`, `needle` and `heart`. Without a `value` (or with null) it shows the indeterminate animation, and new values glide smoothly unless `smooth` is false. Children are drawn over the indicator (a stop button in a circular progress, for example) or, with `border`, inside it, and screen readers announce a progress bar with its percentage. LoaderKit also adds 17 built-in indicators, 50 in total.
